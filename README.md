#Allah, Islam & Historical Origins – A Critical Study

Allah, Islam, and the Deception: Historical Origins and Deities Connected to Allah – A Biblical Discernment

This repository explores the historical origins, development, and theological foundations of Islam. 

It examines primary historical sources, early Islamic texts, and scholarly research regarding the life of Prophet Muhammad, the Quran, and the formation of Islamic doctrine.

Content is presented for educational and research purposes with a critical historical perspective.

All material is for study and discernment.
